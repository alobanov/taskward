Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Taskward is a to-do list for iPhone and Mac made by Aleksei Lobanov. This policy says what happens to what you write in it. In short: nothing you write reaches us.

What we collect

Nothing. Taskward has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else. Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Where your tasks are kept

Your lists, tasks, notes, tags and settings are kept in a database on your device. If iCloud is on for Taskward, they are also kept in your own iCloud account, in its private database, so your iPhone and Mac stay in step. That database belongs to your Apple Account: we cannot see, read or change it. Apple keeps it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Your calendars and reminders

If you let it, Taskward reads the events of your calendars to show them next to your day’s tasks. They are read on your device and are never copied anywhere. Reminders you set are scheduled as notifications by your device itself.

Crashes

If you have chosen to share analytics with Apple, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports for the app. They contain no tasks or other things you have written.

Deleting your data

Delete a task, a list or everything in the app and it is gone from your device and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what it kept on the device. To remove what it kept in iCloud, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, Manage Storage, Taskward, and delete its data.

Children

Taskward collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new one is published here with its date.
