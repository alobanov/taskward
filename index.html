        Built on Getting Things Done

        David Allen’s method for a clear head: get everything out of it and into a trusted place, then decide, one thing at a time. Taskward’s lists are its steps.

        1. CaptureEverything goes into the Inbox, as it comes.
        2. ClarifyWhat is it? Do it now, give it a day, put it off, or keep it as a note.
        3. OrganiseToday, Upcoming, Anytime, Someday, projects and areas.
        4. ReflectLook Someday through now and then; the Logbook keeps what’s done.
        5. EngageWork from one card: Today.

        On first launch a little knight walks you through it with a pile of errands.
