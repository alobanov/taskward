Write it as you’d say it

Type a task with a day in it, the way you would say it, and press Return. The day and the reminder set themselves, and the task lands on its card.

Or say your whole day

Tap the microphone beside Today’s plus and talk: “call the bank at ten, gym in the evening, a present for Anna on Friday.” It comes back as separate tasks with their days and times, and the ones you gave no time are fitted into the gaps between your events. Look them over, then add.

Dictating is part of Pro, on iPhone and Mac.

        Your day by the hour

        Switch the calendar to Day: your events and the tasks with a time lie out by the clock, side by side where they overlap. The free time between them takes one line and says how long it really is, so the whole day fits one screen. The month puts a dot under each day for its events and tasks: tap a day to see it.

        Your calendars are read on the device, never copied. The calendar is part of Pro.

          Every list, a paper card on your desk

          Today lies in front and you work right on it; Upcoming, Anytime and Someday stand behind it in an oak holder. Draw one out and it lies on the desk. Each list has its own paper and a word on what it’s for, so you always know where you are.

          Make it yours

          Light or dark, a palette for each, the desk under the cards, the ink you write in, how a done task is struck through, smooth or toothy paper. Or open a list edge to edge, nothing round it.

          On a Mac too, in step through iCloud

          The same lists on iPhone and Mac, kept in step through your own iCloud: no account to make. On a Mac the holder stands in a window of its own with a sidebar, every list a key away — ⌘1 to ⌘6, ⌘N for a task, ⌘F to find — and with Pro today’s date sits in the menu bar, your day a click under it.
