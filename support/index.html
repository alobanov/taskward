Support

Something not working, or an idea? Write to katleta5000@gmail.com and I’ll answer, usually within a day or two. Say which device and which version of the app you have (Settings, at the foot).

How do my tasks get from iPhone to Mac?

Through your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on both and keep iCloud on for Taskward. If they fall out of step, open the three dots on home and choose Sync Now.

Do smart dates work in my language?

In English and Russian, both at once: “tomorrow at 9am”, “next friday”, “завтра в 9 утра”. Tap the highlighted words if you meant them as words.

How do I see my calendar next to my tasks?

In Settings, turn on calendar events and allow Taskward to read your calendars.

How do I join the beta?

Open the TestFlight invitation on your iPhone or Mac.

How do I delete everything?

See the privacy policy: what to delete in the app, and in iCloud.
